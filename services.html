Service Details

Everything We
Specialize In

Interior Renovations
+
BHI handles full-scope interior renovations for residential clients and property owners. This includes complete kitchen remodels (cabinets, countertops, tile, plumbing rough-in), full bathroom gut renovations, full apartment gut renovations, and apartment unit refreshes between tenants. We manage the entire process — demolition, framing, electrical coordination, plumbing, finishing, and final inspection — so you don't have to juggle multiple contractors.
Building Maintenance
+
Our maintenance programs are designed for property managers and building owners who need consistent, professional upkeep without the headache of managing multiple vendors. We offer scheduled maintenance contracts for private properties and apartment buildings — covering building appearance, trash removal, curb appeal, HVAC filter changes, plumbing inspections, painting touch-ups, flooring repairs, and general property upkeep. Recurring contracts create a predictable maintenance budget and prevent costly emergency repairs.
Painting & Finishing
+

Interior painting is one of our signature services — and we treat it as the skilled trade it is. We perform thorough surface prep (patching, sanding, priming) before any brush touches a wall. Services include full apartment repaints, unit-by-unit interior painting for landlords, and new construction painting.


Apartment Turnover Service — We clean and repaint units when tenants move out, getting your unit back on the market fast and looking its best. We offer two packages:


Basic Package

Standard cleaning and full interior repaint. Walls, ceilings, trim. Unit ready for new tenant move-in. Contact us for pricing details.

Enhanced Package

Deep clean, full interior repaint, minor repairs, and touch-ups throughout the unit. Premium finish ready for listing. Contact us for pricing details.

* Pricing based on unit size. Visit us on Instagram @bhi_bcs or contact us directly for current package pricing.

Plumbing & Heating
+
Full plumbing and heating services including installation, repairs, fixture upgrades, and ongoing maintenance for residential properties throughout New York City. We handle new fixture installations (sinks, toilets, tubs, showers), pipe replacements, leak detection, bathroom and kitchen plumbing rough-ins, water heater installs, and heating system maintenance and repairs.
Flooring
+
BHI installs and refinishes flooring across all major material types: hardwood (new install and refinish), tile, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), marble, and ceramic. We handle subfloor preparation, transitions, and trim so the finished floor looks intentional and lasts. Whether it's a single-room refresh or a multi-unit flooring program, we deliver consistent quality.
Electrical
+
Residential electrical services for property owners and landlords throughout New York City. Services include new electrical installations, panel upgrades, outlet and switch work, lighting fixture installations, electrical repairs, and code compliance updates. As part of a full renovation team, having electrical capabilities under one roof means fewer delays and one point of accountability on your project.
Property Violation Clearance
+
BHI helps property owners remove NYC property violations — quickly, professionally, and with minimal disruption to your tenants or operations. We work through the process on your behalf so you can focus on running your building. * Contact us directly for details on your specific violation type and our clearance process.

What We Do

Full-Service
Building Solutions

BHI Building Services — New York's one-call solution for property owners and landlords who demand quality without compromise. From full-scale renovations and painting to ongoing building maintenance, we handle it so you don't have to coordinate between multiple vendors.

Interior Renovations

Full kitchen and bathroom remodels, full apartment gut renovations, and unit refreshes. We manage every phase from demo to final finish.

Building Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance contracts for private properties and apartment buildings — keeping your building's appearance and cleanliness at the highest standard, including trash removal and curb appeal.

Painting & Finishing

Interior painting with professional surface prep and clean finish work. Includes apartment turnover service — thorough cleaning and repainting of units between tenants.

Plumbing & Heating

Full plumbing and heating services including installation, repairs, fixture upgrades, and system maintenance for residential properties throughout NYC.

Flooring

Hardwood, tile, LVP, marble, and ceramic flooring installation and refinishing. Precise material sourcing and installation built to last.

Electrical

Residential electrical services including new installations, panel upgrades, outlet and fixture work, and electrical repairs for property owners and landlords.

Property Violations

We remove NYC property violations on behalf of property owners — quickly and professionally. Contact us for details.*

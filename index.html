Service Details

Everything We
Specialize In

🏗️   Interior Renovations
+
BHI handles full-scope interior renovations for residential and commercial clients. This includes complete kitchen remodels (cabinets, countertops, tile, plumbing rough-in), full bathroom gut renovations, apartment unit refreshes between tenants, office renovation and buildouts, and industrial space transformations. We manage the entire process — demo, framing, electrical coordination, plumbing, finishing, and final inspection — so you don't have to juggle multiple trades.
🔧   Building Maintenance
+
Our maintenance programs are designed for property managers and building owners who need consistent, professional upkeep without the headache of chasing down multiple vendors. We offer scheduled maintenance contracts covering roof gutters, HVAC filter changes, plumbing inspections, painting touch-ups, flooring repairs, mechanical servicing, and general building upkeep. Recurring contracts create a predictable maintenance budget and prevent costly emergency repairs.
🎨   Painting & Finishing
+
Painting is one of our signature services — and we treat it like the skilled trade it is. We do thorough surface prep (patching, sanding, priming) before any brush hits a wall. Interior and exterior residential painting, commercial painting for offices and retail, full apartment repaints, and new construction painting. We work cleanly, finish sharply, and leave every space looking professional. Our painting work is also one of the most common entry points for clients who go on to complete full renovations with us.
🚿   Plumbing
+
Licensed plumbing services for residential and commercial properties throughout New York City. We handle new fixture installations (sinks, toilets, tubs, showers), plumbing repairs, pipe replacements, leak detection, bathroom and kitchen plumbing rough-ins, and water heater installs. As part of a full renovation team, having in-house plumbing capabilities means fewer delays and one point of accountability on your project.
🏠   Flooring
+
BHI installs and refinishes flooring across all major material types: hardwood (new install and refinish), porcelain and ceramic tile, luxury vinyl plank (LVP), laminate, and commercial-grade flooring systems. We handle subfloor preparation, transitions, and trim — so the finished floor looks intentional, not like an afterthought. Whether it's a single-room refresh or a full building flooring program, we deliver consistent quality at scale.
🏢   Commercial & Industrial Services
+
Our commercial division serves retail storefronts, office buildings, mixed-use properties, warehouses, and facilities management clients. Services include full tenant improvement (TI) buildouts, common area renovations, industrial floor coatings, exterior facade work, commercial painting, and ongoing facility maintenance contracts. We understand the operational requirements of commercial properties — tight timelines, after-hours scheduling, and zero tolerance for disruption to business operations.

Our Story

About BHI

Built on trust, craftsmanship, and an uncompromising commitment to New York's property owners.

10+ Years in NYC
Who We Are

We Build It
Like It's Ours

BHI Building Services was founded on a simple idea: New York property owners deserve a contractor they can actually trust — one that shows up, communicates clearly, does the work right, and stands behind every job.

Founded by Michael Besse and David Pantelides, BHI grew from hands-on renovation work into a full-service building operations company. We built our reputation project by project, client by client — earning repeat business and long-term contracts through consistent execution, not sales pitches.

Today, BHI serves homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial clients across New York City — offering everything from a single bathroom renovation to ongoing maintenance contracts for entire building portfolios. We are not a referral network or a handyman app. We are builders. And when we take on your project, we treat it like it's our own property.

  • Licensed & insured throughout New York State
  • In-house crews — no subcontractor surprises
  • Transparent quotes, no hidden fees
  • Residential and commercial expertise under one roof
  • Recurring maintenance programs available
  • Trusted by property managers across NYC
